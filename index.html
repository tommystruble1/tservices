    04 — Straight answers

    FAQ

    Does any of this work on console?

    No. Everything here is PC only — Rockstar Games Launcher, Steam or Epic. If you're on PS5 or Xbox there's nothing on this page for you, and anyone telling you otherwise is taking your money.

    Will I get banned?

    You might. Rockstar bans for this, and detection changes week to week. Nobody reselling menus can promise you a permanently safe one, us included. Use an account you can afford to lose and go in with your eyes open.

    When do you open?

    Yari is available now. Anything else is still being arranged directly with its developers, and we would rather say "not yet" than put up a product we cannot actually supply. Make an account and you will hear the moment one comes online.

    What am I actually buying?

    A licence key for a menu, plus our time getting it installed and running, plus support afterwards. We resell and support these — we didn't write them, and we don't pretend to.

    Will you mess with other players for me?

    No. We sell menus for your own account. We don't take work aimed at other people's accounts, sessions or connections, and we won't quote for it.

    What if the menu gets detected or goes down?

    Message us. If a menu goes down during your licence period we'll tell you straight away and sort out where you stand — we'd rather lose a sale than have you find out from a ban.

    How do I pay?

    Message tom1x1 and we'll sort the payment method when we confirm your order.
