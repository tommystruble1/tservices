// 06 — INTEL

FAQ

Will this get me banned?

Not if you use it the way it's built. Everything we ship is for offline, single-player or private/community servers. Loading mods into live matchmaking is against every CoD title's terms of service — we won't build for it and we'd advise against it.

Do you do unlock-alls, account boosting or ranked services?

No. No account access, no progression services, no anything that touches another player's experience. If that's what you're after, we're not the right shop.

Console?

PC only. Console modding means modified firmware, and that's a road we don't go down.

How long does a job take?

Small mods land in 24–72 hours. Custom maps run one to three weeks depending on scope. You get a firm date in the quote before you pay anything.

What if it doesn't work on my setup?

We keep revising until it runs on your machine. If we genuinely can't make it work, you get your money back — that's happened twice and both got refunded same day.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. You get full rights to the work, and the source files on Operator and Prestige. We don't resell client builds.

How do I pay?

PayPal Goods & Services or crypto. 50% deposit, 50% on delivery. Invoices on request for server owners who need them.
