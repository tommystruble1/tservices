FAQ

Do you need my account login?

Write this answer yourself before going live. It's the first thing every buyer asks and the whole sale hinges on it. Say exactly what you need from them, whether 2FA has to come off, what you do with the credentials while you work, and what happens to them afterwards. A vague answer here loses more orders than your pricing ever will.

Can I keep playing while you work?

Yes. On trophy orders you can stay online and play whatever you want — the service runs alongside it without interfering. You'll see the trophies popping in the PlayStation app as we go.

How long does each package take?

Base is about 20 minutes and Medium about 35. Large runs roughly 4 hours, and the Max Package takes around 7. Liquid Divinium is quick. Times are honest estimates, not marketing numbers.

Can you prove it works before I pay?

Yes, and we'd rather you asked. Jump in a VC and we'll run the service live on our own account so you can watch it work. Free, no obligation to buy afterwards.

Is there a ban risk?

There is, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you something. None of this is sanctioned by Sony or Activision. Trophy wipes and account action are always possible outcomes. Order on an account you can live without.

Why is the emblem service offline?

The tool is ours and it's mid-update. Emblem orders are on a waitlist right now — get your name down and you'll be at the front of the queue when it's back up.

How do I pay, and do you refund?

Fill in your real terms. Which payment methods you take, whether it's up front or on delivery, and exactly what happens if a job doesn't land. Buyers at these price points decide fast — vague terms read as a red flag.
